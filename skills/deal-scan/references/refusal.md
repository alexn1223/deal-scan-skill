# The refusal

Given verbatim when asked for a verdict — is this a good deal, is the team strong, is the
valuation fair, is the market attractive, is any of this a red flag, should I invest,
should I take the meeting, would you pass:

> This skill collects and cites; it does not judge. I can tell you what each source says,
> who said it, when, and where two sources disagree — but I have no basis for an opinion
> about the company and will not manufacture one. If something in the dossier is thinly
> sourced, say which part and I will go look for more.

Then offer a deep pass on the section behind the question. Do not answer the question in
the same message, do not answer it with a hedge, and do not answer it by arranging facts
so the conclusion is obvious while remaining unstated.

# Registry map

Where to look for legal existence, by jurisdiction. All free at tier 0 unless marked.
Search engines under-serve non-US registries, so reach these by direct fetch rather than
by search.

## Not publicly checkable

Cayman Islands, British Virgin Islands, Seychelles, Marshall Islands, and Singapore
(ACRA, paid) do not offer free public company search. Delaware exposes name and status
only. A company incorporated in one of these is recorded as **not checkable at tier 0** —
which is a statement about the registry, not about the company, and must never be written
up as though a search had failed.

This is desk research assembled from public sources at a single moment in time. It is not
legal due diligence, not an audit, not a background check, not a credit or KYC check, and
not investment advice.

Every line in this dossier is a quotation from a source, with that source linked. The
quotations were verified to appear at those URLs at the fetch timestamps recorded in the
run metadata. Nothing here is a judgment about the company, the people, the market or the
opportunity, and nothing here should be read as one.

Sources go stale, pages change, and absence from the sources searched is not evidence
that a thing does not exist. Anything that matters to a decision should be confirmed with
the company, with counsel, or with the issuing authority before that decision is made.
